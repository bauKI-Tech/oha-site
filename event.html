— what this is

A small, considered afternoon. Not a launch party. A first class. First community.

For thirteen years ohá was a clothing label, a Turkish blog, a name readers wrote back to me. On 28 June it becomes a quieter thing, a house of objects for the body. The mat. The clip. The grip. The towel. The shopper. Five objects, to begin.

This afternoon is how we begin. Forty people on forty mats in a room full of day light. One class led by a certified instructor I trust. After class, we slow down together, sharing Turkish coffee, matcha, and healthy snacks around a long oak table. A moment to connect, unwind, and enjoy positive energy, meaningful conversations, and a beautiful memory together.

No launch noise. No press. No countdown. Just a small group, a quiet room, and the new ohá in your hands by the end of it.

If you have been here from the beginning, thank you. If you have just arrived, welcome.

— the class

Powerful, controlled, intentional.

A 55-minute mat Pilates class designed to challenge the entire body through strength, endurance, control, posture, breath, and deep muscular connection. This is a powerful full-body practice that pushes you to explore the potential of your body with precision and purpose.

Every movement is guided by breath, alignment, control, and feeling, allowing you to move with awareness and connect more deeply to your body.

Ohá welcomes both beginners and lifelong practitioners alike. No experience required. No performance expected. No mirrors at the front of the room. You will leave feeling stronger, taller, lighter, and more connected than when you arrived.

If you are pregnant, recovering from surgery, or working with an injury, please write to us beforehand. The instructor will thoughtfully adapt the practice to support you, never asking your body to move beyond what it needs.

questions.

Do I need to know Pilates?
No, you'll be guided throughout the entire class. This could even be the start of something new for you.
What should I bring?
You don't need to bring anything, just a positive vibe and all-black Pilates or yoga wear. Grip socks and a small workout towel will be provided by us. Please arrive already dressed for class, as there are no changing rooms at the studio.
Can I bring a friend?
Of course, you're always welcome to come together. Your friend will simply need to book a spot for the Pilates class as well. As long as spots are available, bookings remain open.
Can I get a refund?
Cancellations can be made up to 48 hours before the event by emailing contact@shopoha.com. You will then receive a full refund, and your spot will be released to someone else. Unfortunately, cancellations made less than 48 hours before the event are non-refundable.
What if I have an injury or I'm pregnant?
That's completely okay, we simply ask you to let the instructor know before the class begins. At the start of the session, the instructor will also check in with the group to see if there's anything that should be taken into consideration during the practice. Because this is a powerful full-body class, we kindly advise against joining if you are more than five months pregnant.
Will there be photography?
Yes, photos and videos will be taken during the event for social media and brand purposes. By purchasing a Pilates class spot, you agree to the use of this content across OHÁ channels and platforms.
