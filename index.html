— what it was

A small, considered afternoon. Not a launch party. A first class. A first community. Exactly as promised.

Forty people on forty mats in a room full of daylight. One guided class, then Turkish coffee, matcha, and a goodie bag with the new ohá inside. To the forty who were there — thank you for beginning with us. To everyone who watched from afar: the story is only starting.
